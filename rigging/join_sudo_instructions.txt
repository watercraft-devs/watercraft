Verify your username FIRST:

	`whoami`

example response username: john

That's the username you add to the sudo group.


Drop into the root shell:


	`su -`

Ensure you are updated to latest packages:

	`apt update && apt upgrade -y`

Add your username to the sudo group:

	`adduser john sudo`

Ensure 'john' is replaced with the username you can verify in the first step (BEFORE YOU DROP INTO ROOT)
